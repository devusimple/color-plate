How to Apply the 60-30-10 Rule

A practical guide to distributing your color palette across UI components for a cohesive, professional design.

60% Dominant
30% Secondary
10% Accent
60%

Dominant Color

  • Background / Surface colors
  • Main content area
  • Header & Footer backgrounds
  • Card & container backgrounds
  • Modal & overlay backdrops
  • Body text (on light dominant)
30%

Secondary Color

  • Sidebar & navigation bars
  • Input fields & form controls
  • Card borders & dividers
  • Secondary buttons
  • Table row backgrounds
  • Avatar & icon backgrounds
10%

Accent Color

  • Primary action buttons
  • Links & interactive states
  • Notification badges & icons
  • Active / selected states
  • CTA banners & highlights
  • Progress bars & sliders

Don't Break the Ratio

The 60-30-10 rule creates visual balance. If accent color overwhelms the secondary, reduce its usage or lighten its intensity.

🔄

Context Matters

The rule applies to each screen / page, not the entire app. Dark mode may invert the roles of dominant and secondary.

🎨

Use Shades & Tints

Create depth by using lighter or darker tints of your three base colors. They still count toward the same 60-30-10 bucket.

♿

Check Contrast Ratios

Ensure text on dominant backgrounds meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 minimum). Use white text on dark, dark text on light.
